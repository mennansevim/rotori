# App Store Review Notes — Premium Pocket Translator

## English (paste into App Review Information)

Pocket Translator is available from any trip plan: open a plan and select the
Japanese tab. The card appears at the top, before the free ready-to-use phrases.

Entitlement behavior:

- Free users see the Pocket Translator card in a locked state. The ready-to-use
  phrase categories below it remain available.
- Premium users see the two-way Turkish/English ↔ Japanese text translator.
- The translator reacts immediately when the shared Premium entitlement changes.

First-time Premium setup:

1. Tap “Download language packs” while online. This downloads the Japanese and
   Turkish/English Google ML Kit translation models.
2. Enter text, choose the direction, and tap “Translate with Google”.
3. After the language models are installed, both directions can be tested in
   Airplane Mode.

Privacy:

- Translation input and output are processed on the device and are not sent to
  Rotori servers, stored, or logged.
- Pocket Translator does not use the microphone or speech recognition.
- The Google translation attribution appears next to every result.
